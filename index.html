What is Obsidian?

  • Obsidian is a very strong block in Minecraft, known for its blast resistance.
  • Formed when water touches a lava source block.
  • Can only be mined with a diamond or netherite pickaxe.
  • Takes ~9.4 seconds to mine with a diamond pickaxe (without enchantments).
  • Used to build Nether Portals.
  • Required for crafting an Enchanting Table and Ender Chest.
  • Cannot be destroyed by normal explosions (e.g., creepers, TNT).
  • Ender Dragon can still destroy it in The End.
  • Hardness level: 50 (one of the highest in the game).
  • Players often use obsidian to build blast-proof bases in PvP servers.
  • Not affected by pistons or other movement mechanics.
  • Black in color with a purple shimmer.
  • Stackable in groups of 64.
